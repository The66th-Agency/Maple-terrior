Delivery and Pickup

How much does delivery cost, and how long does it take?

We pack every order within 1 to 3 business days. Standard delivery then takes 3 to 9 business days for $19.99, Express takes 1 to 2 business days for $39.99, and orders of $99 or more ship free.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver online orders to all ten Canadian provinces. Yukon, the Northwest Territories, Nunavut and addresses outside Canada sit outside our delivery zone, so the checkout offers no option for them. For one of those addresses, email info@mapleterroir.com before you order and we will tell you whether we can send it.

Can I pick up my order in Vancouver?

Yes. Choose pickup at checkout and collect your order at 9291 Shaughnessy Street in Vancouver, at no charge. It is the same facility that packs every online order.

Where is my order packed?

Every online order is packed at our facility at 9291 Shaughnessy Street in Vancouver. The facility holds a federal Safe Food for Canadians licence, which the Canadian Food Inspection Agency requires of businesses that make, package or label food sent across provincial borders (CFIA, 2026).

Sending a Gift

Can I send an order straight to someone else?

Yes. At checkout, enter the recipient's name and address as the shipping address and your own card details for payment. The box goes to them, and the order confirmation comes to the email address you enter.

Can I add a gift message or gift wrap?

Our checkout has no gift message field and no gift wrap. Our gift sets and home sets arrive in their own boxes, and First Tap Nouveau syrup comes in its own gift box. Send the recipient a text or a card of your own so they know who it is from.

When should I order for Christmas?

Order by December 10 for Christmas delivery to any province. Toronto, our longest route, takes about seven days. In Vancouver, you can pick up your order at 9291 Shaughnessy Street until December 23.

Which gift set should I send?

Our eight sets run from $75 to $240, and each set's page lists what is inside. For one person, the $75 Maple Extravaganza Gift Set holds organic maple syrup with tea, stroopwafels, shortbread, popcorn and nuts. For a family that cooks, the Maple Treat Home Set, also $75, adds organic maple butter and organic maple sugar to the syrup.

Damaged Items and Returns

What if something arrives damaged?

Email info@mapleterroir.com or call +1 (604) 324-6556 within 7 days of delivery, and we will replace the item or refund it to your original payment method. A photo of the item and the box helps us sort it out on the first reply.

Can I return something I changed my mind about?

We sell food, so we accept returns only for damaged or defective items. Opened or partly eaten products and sale items are final. If a product disappoints you, tell us anyway: we read every message.

How do I send a damaged item back?

Email us first, then send the item unused, in its original packaging, with your receipt or order number, to Maple Terroir Products Ltd, 9291 Shaughnessy Street, Vancouver, BC V6P 6P4. The full terms are in our refund policy.

Paying and Team Orders

How can I pay?

Our checkout takes Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover and Diners Club, plus Shop Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Every price on the site and at checkout is in Canadian dollars.

Is there a lower price for a team or client order?

Yes. Many of our products come in packs of 12, 24, 36 and 48 at 10, 15, 20 and 25 percent off, and each product page lists its packs. A 250 mL bottle of our organic syrup is $14.99 alone and $11.25 each in a pack of 48. See our corporate gift page for staff and client gifts, or our wholesale page for stores and cafes.

Our Food

Where does your maple syrup come from, and where are the treats made?

Our maple syrup comes from one farm in the Appalachian Mountains of Quebec. Our stroopwafels are made in Ontario, and our maple blueberry cookies, maple sugar cookies, maple coffee and maple popcorn are made in Vancouver.

Which products are certified organic?

Our organic 250 mL syrup and our First Tap Nouveau syrup are certified organic by Ecocert, Canada Organic and USDA Organic. They are the only products we sell with that certification, and each product page lists its ingredients. Our certifications page shows each one.

What can I send someone with a peanut allergy?

Our Maple Leaf Mini Shortbread Cookies are peanut-free and made in a peanut-free facility. The maple and sea salt peanuts, the chocolate maple almonds and the gift sets that hold them contain nuts. For anything else, read the ingredients on its product page before you order.

How do I store maple syrup once it arrives?

Keep unopened bottles in a cool, dry cupboard. Once a bottle is open, keep it in the fridge and use it within 6 months; our shelf life checker gives the date for your bottle.
