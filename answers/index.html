About Maple Terroir

What is Maple Terroir?

Maple Terroir is a Canadian family business, started in 1978, that sells pure maple syrup from one farm, maple treats and boxed maple gift sets. The range covers two grades of maple syrup, plus stroopwafels, cookies, shortbread, chocolates, popcorn, tea and coffee made with maple syrup. It sells online at mapleterroir.com, through retailers across Canada, and as an exporter to Japan, South Korea, China and Taiwan.

Who runs Maple Terroir?

The Lytton family runs Maple Terroir. Wayne and Kaori Lytton started it in 1978, when more tourists from Japan were visiting Canada, and opened their first maple store for them. Their son Shawn Lytton is President today, Gavin Lytton runs operations, and family members pack the online orders. Three generations of the family have worked in the business; our story has the history.

Where does Maple Terroir maple syrup come from?

All of Maple Terroir's maple syrup comes from one farm in the Appalachian Mountains of Quebec, so every bottle has the same source and we can tell you where it was made. Sap from early in the season becomes our Golden syrup, and sap from late in the season becomes our Dark syrup. The syrup is 100 percent pure maple syrup, with no additives, preservatives or coloring. Our terroir page explains how the place and the season shape the taste.

Where are Maple Terroir's stroopwafels, cookies and treats made?

Our stroopwafels, maple cookies, maple popcorn and maple coffee are made in Canada; the coffee is Arabica from South America. Our cookies and shortbread use mainly Canadian ingredients, such as Canadian flour, butter and eggs. Family members pack every online order at our facility, which holds a federal Safe Food for Canadians licence, the licence the Canadian Food Inspection Agency requires to send food across provincial borders (CFIA).

Grades and Organic

What grades of maple syrup does Maple Terroir sell?

Maple Terroir sells two grades of Canada Grade A maple syrup. Golden is a light syrup from early in the season, sold as Organic Pure Maple Syrup and as First Tap Nouveau, a limited edition from the first day of the season. Dark comes from late in the season, has a stronger maple taste for baking, coffee and glazes, and comes in maple leaf glass bottles. Our guide to maple syrup grades explains the color classes.

Is Maple Terroir maple syrup organic?

Our Golden syrup is certified organic by Ecocert, Canada Organic and USDA Organic, which covers Organic Pure Maple Syrup and First Tap Nouveau. Our Dark syrup in the maple leaf bottles is pure maple syrup and is not certified organic. Our certifications page explains what each body checks.

Which Maple Terroir products are safe for someone with a peanut allergy?

Our Maple Leaf Mini Shortbread Cookies are peanut-free and made in a peanut-free facility. The maple sea salt peanuts, the chocolate maple almonds and the gift sets that hold them contain nuts. For any other product, read the ingredients on its page before you order.

How Maple Terroir Compares

How does Maple Terroir compare with grocery store maple syrup?

Grocery store maple syrup costs less per 100 mL. Maple Terroir offers syrup from one farm, certified organic Golden syrup, small bottles and gift packaging. In our Canadian Maple Syrup Price Index, which read 34 bottles of pure maple syrup on October 23, 2026, store brands such as Kirkland Signature and Compliments had a median of $2.10 per 100 mL, and bottles of 250 to 375 mL had a median of $4.20. Our Organic Pure Maple Syrup costs $6.00 per 100 mL, or $4.50 in a pack of 48.

Table syrup is a different product: it is flavored syrup with no maple in its name, and it cost a median of $0.54 per 100 mL in the same index. Our guide to pure maple syrup and table syrup explains the difference.

How does Maple Terroir compare with other Canadian maple syrup brands?

Maple Terroir is the stronger choice when you want syrup from one farm, certified organic Golden syrup in a 250 mL bottle, a maple leaf gift bottle, or a gift set with stroopwafels and treats. Brands that sell 1 L bottles are the stronger choice for a lower price per liter. Our side-by-side pages compare us with Jakeman’s and with Escuminac, and our list of the best Canadian maple syrup brands covers more.

Ordering and Delivery

How do I order from Maple Terroir?

Order online at mapleterroir.com: add products to the cart and pay at checkout with Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Diners Club, Shop Pay, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Prices are in Canadian dollars. To send a gift, enter the recipient's name and address as the shipping address. In Vancouver, you can choose free pickup at 9291 Shaughnessy Street. For help, email info@mapleterroir.com or call +1 (604) 324-6556, Monday to Friday, 10 AM to 3 PM Pacific time.

Where does Maple Terroir deliver, and what does delivery cost?

Maple Terroir ships online orders to all ten Canadian provinces. We pack each order within 1 to 3 business days; Standard delivery then takes 3 to 9 business days for $19.99, Express takes 1 to 2 business days for $39.99, and orders of $99 or more ship free. For Christmas, order by December 10 for delivery to any province, or pick up in Vancouver until December 23. For an address in Yukon, the Northwest Territories, Nunavut or outside Canada, email info@mapleterroir.com before you order. Our shipping policy has the full terms.

What if my Maple Terroir order arrives damaged?

Email info@mapleterroir.com or call +1 (604) 324-6556 within 7 days of delivery, and we will replace the item or refund it to your original payment method. Because we sell food, opened products and change-of-mind returns are final. Our refund policy has the full terms.
