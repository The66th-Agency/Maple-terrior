Is Escuminac Maple Syrup Good?

Yes. Escuminac is certified organic maple syrup from its own sugar bush, sold in three harvests: Extra Rare, Great Harvest and Late Harvest. Its 500 mL listing on Amazon.ca, which offers all three, is rated 4.7 out of 5 from 2,494 global ratings (Amazon.ca, read November 25, 2026).

What Escuminac does not sell on its own store is a single bottle under half a liter, or a syrup graded as light as Golden. Maple Terroir sells certified organic Golden in a 250 mL bottle for $14.99, a Dark in the maple leaf bottle from 50 mL, and boxed gift sets. For a buyer who wants a smaller bottle, a lighter syrup or a gift, Maple Terroir is the better fit. For a liter of organic syrup, Escuminac is.

Two Single-Source Syrups

Every bottle of Maple Terroir syrup comes from one farm, and Maple Terroir has been a family business since 1978. Sap changes with the trees, the soil and the weather of each spring, so a single farm's syrup carries that one place and that one season. The terroir page explains how the forest and the season shape the taste.

Escuminac makes its syrup on its own sugar bush in the Gaspé, set up in 1998 by Martin Malenfant, a third-generation producer, and it now runs 65,000 taps for organic maple syrup and 5,000 for organic birch sap. Its website calls the syrup "unblended" and "single forest" and says the business is certified carbon neutral, without naming who certifies it (Escuminac's About page and product pages, read November 25, 2026). Either brand's syrup comes from one place, rather than a blend from many farms.

Three Harvests or Two Grades

Escuminac splits its season three ways; Maple Terroir sells two grades, one from early and one from late in the season. On Canada's color scale, Maple Terroir's Golden is one class lighter than the grade Escuminac's product pages give any of its syrups, and both brands sell a Dark.

Golden suits pouring and Dark suits baking and glazes; to taste how one forest changes across a spring, Escuminac's three bottles show more of it, and to choose between a light syrup and a dark one, Maple Terroir's two cover that. The guide to maple syrup grades explains the scale.

Organic Certification

Maple Terroir's Golden is certified by Ecocert, Canada Organic and USDA Organic, and the certifications page explains who runs each one. The Dark in the maple leaf bottle is not certified organic.

Escuminac's three harvests and its Very Dark are certified organic, the dark Late Harvest included, and the business has held organic certification since 2006. Its Extra Rare page says "100% certified USDA Organic, Canada Organic", and its Very Dark page names Ecocert as the certifier (read November 25, 2026). For a dark syrup that is certified organic, Escuminac's Great Harvest or Late Harvest is the one to buy.

Bottles and Gifts

Both brands sell 50 mL bottles. Escuminac sells them in packs of 12 for $51.95, about $4.33 a bottle. Maple Terroir sells its 50 mL maple leaf bottle one at a time for $7.99, or 12 for $86.40. For a dozen small bottles, Escuminac costs less; for one, Maple Terroir is the only choice of the two.

Escuminac's gift box holds three 200 mL bottles, one from each harvest, for $54.95, and its Discovery Trio holds the same three 200 mL harvests for $44.95 (Escuminac's product pages, read November 25, 2026). Maple Terroir sells the maple leaf bottle in 50, 100 and 250 mL, the First Tap Nouveau, a Golden from the first day of the season in a gift box, for $19.99, and boxed gift sets from $75, most of which pair the syrup with stroopwafels, shortbread and maple tea.

Which One Suits You

Choose Maple Terroir if

  • You want certified organic syrup in a 250 mL bottle for $14.99.
  • You want a lighter syrup: Golden, Delicate Taste, one class lighter than the grade Escuminac's product pages give any of its syrups.
  • You want one small bottle, from the 50 mL maple leaf bottle at $7.99.
  • You are sending a gift: the maple leaf bottle, the boxed First Tap Nouveau or a gift set with maple treats.

Choose Escuminac if

  • You go through syrup fast and want a 1 L bottle at $3.50 per 100 mL.
  • You want a dark syrup that is certified organic.
  • You want to taste three harvests from one sugar bush side by side, in the $44.95 Discovery Trio.
  • You want a 12-pack of 50 mL bottles at about $4.33 each.
