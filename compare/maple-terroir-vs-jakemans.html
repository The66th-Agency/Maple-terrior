One Farm or Many Farms

Jakeman's buys syrup from producers across Ontario and blends it. Its product pages describe syrup "sourced from local farms across Ontario," including Mennonite communities around St. Jacobs and sugar bushes in Northern Ontario, and its organic page calls the result an "award-winning blend" (Jakeman's pure maple syrup, Jakeman's organic maple syrup). Blending many farms gives a steady taste from bottle to bottle and keeps a large grocery supply on the shelf.

Every bottle of ours comes from one forest on one farm. Sap changes with the trees, the soil and the weather of each spring, so a single farm's syrup carries that one place and that one season. Our terroir page explains what shapes the taste, and why early syrup comes out golden and late syrup dark.

Organic Certification

Both brands sell a certified organic syrup. Our Golden grade is certified by Ecocert, Canada Organic and USDA Organic, and our certifications page explains what each body checks. Our Dark grade in the maple leaf bottle is not certified organic.

Jakeman's sells organic syrup as its own line, labeled "Organic/BIO certified," in 250, 370 and 500 mL glass (Jakeman's organic maple syrup). The product page does not name the certifying body. On October 19, 2026 its 500 mL bottle was $23.99 and the 250 mL was sold out.

Grades and Taste

We sell two grades from the same farm, picked by when in the season the sap ran. Golden comes from early in the season and has a delicate taste for pouring. Dark comes from late in the season and has a stronger maple flavor that holds up in baking and glazes. Our guide to maple syrup grades explains the scale.

Jakeman's sells one Canada Grade A syrup across its main line, in every size from 50 mL to 1 L, with no early or late grade to pick between. If you want to choose between a light syrup and a dark one, our two bottles give you that choice.
