Questions About Gift Baskets in Edmonton

The sets cost $75 to $240 CAD. Shipping to an Edmonton address is $19.99 for Standard, which takes 3 to 9 business days, or $39.99 for Express, which takes 1 to 2, and an order of $99 CAD or more ships free. Sherwood Park, St. Albert, Spruce Grove, Leduc, Red Deer, Grande Prairie and Fort McMurray get the same two options at the same prices. Nothing arrives the same day.

Most baskets sold in Edmonton are assembled from other brands' products. Ours hold one family's own maple range: syrup from a single farm, and the stroopwafels, shortbread, popcorn, tea and chocolate almonds made with it. Each product page lists every item, so the person receiving the box can see exactly what is inside.

Hampers, yes, packed as a box of maple foods. We pack no fresh fruit, cheese, flowers or alcohol. If the person was expecting a fruit basket, the maple sets give them a cupboard of sealed treats instead, from organic syrup and maple butter to stroopwafels and shortbread.

Put their name and address in the shipping fields at checkout and your own details in the billing fields. One order goes to one address, so a second person needs a second order. The checkout has no gift message, so tell them a parcel is on its way.

Order by December 7 for a box under the tree on December 25. That date leaves a margin on the slower option, and weekends do not count as business days, so they add to the wait.
