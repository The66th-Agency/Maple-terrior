Writing a Calgary Address So the Box Arrives

The City of Calgary writes every standard address with a quadrant at the end: NE, NW, SE or SW. The quadrant tells the driver which part of the city the street sits in, so type it in the street line when you check out.

The quadrant goes last

The City's own examples read 625 25 Av SE and 800 Macleod Tr SW. Copy the address exactly as the person you are sending to writes it, quadrant included.

A suite number comes first

For an apartment, condo or office tower, the City puts the suite ahead of the street number, as in #100 2320 1 St SW. You can also add it on the second address line at checkout.

One order, one address

Each order ships to a single address. To send boxes to two people in Calgary, or to a parent in Calgary and a sibling in Edmonton, place one order for each. Put the recipient's name on the order, not yours.

When to Order for the Dates Calgary Gives Gifts

Thanksgiving

Alberta lists Thanksgiving Day as a general holiday on the second Monday of October, which falls on October 12 in 2026. A box of tea, shortbread and syrup makes a hostess gift for the dinner. The Monday is a holiday, so plan around the business days before it: order Standard at least two weeks out, or Express by the Wednesday before.

Christmas

Standard can take up to nine business days, close to two working weeks, so we suggest a Standard order no later than December 7 for a box to reach Calgary before Christmas. After that date, choose Express at checkout. Weekends do not count as business days, so they add to the wait in either case.
