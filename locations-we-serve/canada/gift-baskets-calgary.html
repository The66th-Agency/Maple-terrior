Questions About Gift Baskets in Calgary

Our gift sets cost $75 to $240 CAD. A $75 set with Standard shipping comes to $94.99 at checkout, or $114.99 with Express. Any order of $99 CAD or more ships to Calgary free, so every set from $100 up arrives with no shipping charge.

No. We pack and ship every order from Vancouver, so the fastest option is Express, at 1 to 2 business days. Standard takes 3 to 9 business days. Order a few days ahead of the date.

We sell maple gift hampers packed in a box, not fresh fruit. Each set holds packaged maple foods such as syrup, tea, shortbread, stroopwafels, popcorn and chocolate almonds, and each product page lists the contents.

Enter their name and Calgary address as the shipping address at checkout, with the quadrant (NE, NW, SE or SW) at the end of the street line and any suite number in front. Each order ships to one address, so place a separate order for each person.

For Standard shipping, which can take up to nine business days, we suggest ordering by December 7. After that, choose Express, which takes 1 to 2 business days. Weekends are not business days, so they add to the wait.
