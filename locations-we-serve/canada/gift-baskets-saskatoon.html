Getting It to the Right Saskatoon Door

You place the order here and we send the box to them. Three things on the order make sure it reaches the person and they know who sent it.

  1. 1

    Send it where they are this week
    If someone is staying with family after a loss or a hospital stay, use that address, not their own.

  2. 2

    Their name and address under shipping, yours under billing
    Each order goes to one address, so a box for two households is two orders.

  3. 3

    Send the text from the set you chose
    The checkout has no gift message, so your text is the card.
