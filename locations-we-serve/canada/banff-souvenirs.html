Flying Home From Calgary: What Goes Where

Banff and Lake Louise Tourism names Calgary International Airport as the closest international airport, about a 90-minute drive from the park. If you fly home from there, the Canadian Air Transport Security Authority (CATSA) screens your bags, and it sorts maple souvenirs this way.

  1. 1

    Cookies, stroopwafels, chocolates and tea can ride in either bag. They stay solid at room temperature, which is how CATSA defines solid food, and CATSA allows solid food in carry-on and checked baggage.

  2. 2

    Syrup over 100 mL and maple butter over 100 g go in the checked bag. CATSA counts maple syrup as a liquid and maple spread as a gel, and lets them into a carry-on only in containers of 100 mL or 100 g or less, all inside one clear 1 L bag.

  3. 3

    Or leave the syrup out of your luggage. CATSA names maple syrup among the gifts to keep out of a carry-on and suggests a checked bag or shipping it separately, which we can do for you.

Driving home instead? Then you can keep the bottle in the car. Carry-on rules from CATSA's pages on liquids and non-solid food and on maple syrup, checked October 3, 2026. CATSA can change them, so check before you fly.
