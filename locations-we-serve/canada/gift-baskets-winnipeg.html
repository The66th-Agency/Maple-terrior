Sending a Box Into a Prairie Winter

In a Winnipeg winter, the best gift box is the one that goes straight indoors. Three things settle it before you pay.

  1. 1

    Send it where someone takes it in
    A workplace with a front desk, a condo with a parcel room, or a house where someone is home in the day. For a house with a front porch, ask them to check it.

  2. 2

    Use their address and yours
    Their name, the company and the suite or unit go under shipping; your own details go under billing.

  3. 3

    Tell them it is coming
    The checkout carries no gift card, so a text from you says who sent it and to watch for the box.
