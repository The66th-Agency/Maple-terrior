Packing Maple for the Flight Home

CATSA, the agency that screens passengers at Canadian airports, treats solid food and syrup differently, so sort your souvenirs into two piles before you pack.

In your carry-on

Cookies, chocolate and nuts are solid food, and CATSA lets you carry them onto the plane with no size limit.

In your checked bag

In a carry-on, CATSA allows syrup only in containers of 100 mL or less, all inside one clear, resealable 1 L bag, and asks travelers to pack maple syrup bought as a gift in checked baggage. The syrups we have in stock come in 250 mL bottles, and maple butter is a spread, so both go in the checked bag.
