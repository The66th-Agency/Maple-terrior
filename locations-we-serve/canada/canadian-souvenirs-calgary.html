What Gets Through Security at YYC

Every passenger at Calgary International Airport goes through screening by the Canadian Air Transport Security Authority (CATSA). Syrup counts as a liquid there and maple butter as a gel, which means the size of each container picks its bag. We sorted our range that way using CATSA's guidance on maple syrup, liquids and non-solid food and solid food, checked November 6, 2026. The rules can change, so look again before your flight.

In the clear bag

Each container 100 mL or less. They all share one clear, resealable bag of up to 1 L per passenger, and the bag comes out of your carry-on at screening.

Loose in your carry-on

Solid food can travel in either bag when you fly inside Canada, so boxed cookies, wafers and nuts can sit anywhere in your backpack.

In your checked bag

Anything larger than 100 mL or 100 g. For us that means the 250 mL bottle, the 160 g jar of maple butter and the gift set, which holds a 250 mL bottle. CATSA suggests the checked bag for any souvenir that holds syrup.

Connecting to a flight outside Canada? Once you land in another country, the food in both of your bags follows that country's rules, so look them up before you pack.
