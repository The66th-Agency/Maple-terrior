Sending It to Someone

How to Send a Gift Set to Someone in Toronto

You order on mapleterroir.com, and we ship the box straight to the person you are giving it to. You can help it reach their door with the details you type into the address at checkout, especially if they live in a condo or apartment building.

  1. 1

    Ship it to them, not to you

    At checkout, enter the recipient's full name, as it appears on their mail, and their Toronto address as the shipping address.

  2. 2

    Add the unit number

    For a condo or apartment, put the unit or suite number in the address. Without it, the box can reach the building but not the person.

  3. 3

    Tell them a box is on the way

    If their building holds parcels at a concierge desk or in a parcel room, they will know to check there.

  4. 4

    Pick the speed for the date

    Standard takes 3 to 9 business days. If the birthday or holiday is less than two weeks away, choose Express, which takes 1 to 2.

Gifts for Staff and Clients

Ordering Maple Gifts for a Toronto Office

For a shared office kitchen, the Pure Maple Syrup Stroopwafels caddy holds 1,190 g of stroopwafels, each wrapped on its own, for $39.99 CAD. For one gift per person, or for sets going to several addresses, email info@mapleterroir.com with the number of sets you need.

If the gifts are for your employees, keep the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) rule on gifts in mind when you set the budget. Under its administrative policy, the CRA does not treat non-cash gifts for a special occasion, such as a religious holiday or a birthday, as a taxable benefit when the gifts to one employee add up to $500 or less in the year, taxes included. The CRA taxes the amount over $500, and it does not apply this policy to cash, to near-cash gifts such as a prepaid Visa card, or to gifts for a relative or anyone else you do not deal with at arm's length. Read the CRA page on gifts and awards, or ask your accountant, before you decide.
