Two Bottles Fit the Cabin. One Rides in the Hold.

Security at Montréal-Trudeau is run by the Canadian Air Transport Security Authority (CATSA). It lets syrup into a carry-on only in a container of 100 mL or less, inside your one clear 1 L bag. Our 50 mL and 100 mL leaf bottles are under that line. The 250 mL bottle goes in your suitcase.
