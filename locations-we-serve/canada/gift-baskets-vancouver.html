Questions About Gift Baskets in Vancouver

Our gift sets cost $75 to $240 CAD. Shipping to a Vancouver address is $19.99 for Standard or $39.99 for Express, and any order of $99 CAD or more ships free. If you collect the box at our location on Shaughnessy Street, you pay the set price and nothing else.

Not delivered. Express is our fastest shipping, at 1 to 2 business days. If you need it sooner, order online, choose local pickup at checkout and collect it at 9291 Shaughnessy Street; the checkout tells you when the order is ready.

We sell maple hampers packed in a box, with no fresh fruit, cheese or wine. The boxes hold sealed maple foods such as organic syrup, maple butter, stroopwafels, shortbread, popcorn, tea and chocolate almonds, and each product page lists every item.

At checkout, enter their name and address as the shipping address, with the unit number on the apartment line if they live in an apartment or a condo. Each order ships to one address, so place one order for each person. Our checkout has no gift message, so let them know a parcel is coming.

For Christmas on December 25, order by December 7 for Standard shipping or by December 22 for Express. For Lunar New Year on February 6, 2027, order by January 22 for Standard or by February 3 for Express. The checkout counts business days only, so weekends add to the wait.
