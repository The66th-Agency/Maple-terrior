Sending a Box to a Student at Dalhousie

A parcel for someone in residence goes to the building's mail service first, then to the student. Dalhousie publishes the exact address layout it wants from families, shown on the label here, and the three steps below get the box from our checkout into the right hands.

  1. 1

    Copy the residence address line by line
    Their room number or house goes in the apartment field at checkout, and the PO Box goes on the second address line. For a flat or a house off campus, the unit number is the one line that matters most.

  2. 2

    Their name for shipping, your name for billing
    Dalhousie's layout starts with the student's full name, so use the name they registered under, and keep your own details for the receipt.

  3. 3

    Tell them to watch for it
    There is no card inside, so a quick message saying who sent it and to check residence mail does the job a card would.
