Maple Terroir's Maple Stroopwafel Waffle Cookies are a Canadian-style version of the Dutch stroopwafel, with a filling made with pure maple syrup! 

Maple Terroir's Maple Syrup Stroopwafels are made mainly from Canadian ingredients and are simply delicious! Many stroopwafel brands use glucose-fructose as their first ingredient to cut costs. In comparison, our stroopwafels use flour as the first ingredient. We also don't use generic vegetable oil, margarine, fillers, white sugar, and artificial flavours like other brands do. Instead, our stroopwafels are made with butter, eggs, cane sugar, and other natural ingredients, including pure maple syrup.Not only does this make for healthier and tastier stroopwafels, but it also allows us to do our part in supporting and using local dairy ingredients.

  • Non-GMO ingredients
  • The first ingredient is Canadian-grown flour
  • Canadian eggs
  • Canadian sea salt
  • Sustainable palm oil
  • Cane sugar
  • No preservatives, artificial flavouring or citric acid
  • Sweetened with pure maple syrup
  • A great source of energy for athletes or anyone wanting a pick-me-up on the go!
  • This product is Kosher

Frequently Asked Questions

Keep maple syrup and maple cream in the fridge once opened, and unopened bottles in a cool, dry place. For maple sugar, cookies, chocolates, snacks, tea and coffee, follow the storage note on the package.
Our organic maple syrups, the Organic Pure Maple Syrup 250ml and the Organic First Tap Nouveau, are certified organic by Ecocert, Canada Organic and USDA Organic. The maple leaf bottles and the 100 mL jug are not organic, and our cookies, stroopwafels, chocolates, snacks, tea and coffee are not certified organic.
Our online checkout ships to the ten Canadian provinces, and shipping is free on orders of $99 CAD or more. For an address in the territories or another country, email info@mapleterroir.com before you order and we will confirm whether we can ship there and what it costs.
If an item arrives damaged or defective, email info@mapleterroir.com within 7 days of delivery and we will replace it or refund it. Because these are food products, we cannot accept returns of opened items or returns for a change of mind.
Pure maple syrup, maple sugar and maple cream are made from maple syrup alone. Our stroopwafels, cookies, chocolates and snacks may contain wheat, dairy, eggs, soy and nuts. Please check the label on each product for allergen information, or email info@mapleterroir.com before you order.
All our maple syrup is Canada Grade A. The organic 250 mL bottle and the First Tap Nouveau are Golden, Delicate Taste. The maple leaf bottles and the 100 mL jug are Dark, Robust Taste, with a stronger maple flavor.
