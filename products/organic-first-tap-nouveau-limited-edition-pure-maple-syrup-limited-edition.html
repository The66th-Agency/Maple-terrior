We are proud to introduce First Tap Organic Maple Syrup Nouveau. The initial piercing or "First Tap" of the tree, allows the sap to run freely, which is then collected. This concentrated sap is immediately boiled in large evaporators, bottled then labeled all in the first day of the maple crop season. Each bottle of our First Tap Maple Syrup receives its own serial number from the total limited amount that is collected on that first day to show its authenticity. First Tap Maple Syrup can only be Grade A Golden, Delicate Taste. Enjoy this one-of-a-kind Pure Maple Syrup from our Sugar House to your table!

Frequently Asked Questions

Keep maple syrup and maple cream in the fridge once opened, and unopened bottles in a cool, dry place. For maple sugar, cookies, chocolates, snacks, tea and coffee, follow the storage note on the package.
Our organic maple syrups, the Organic Pure Maple Syrup 250ml and the Organic First Tap Nouveau, are certified organic by Ecocert, Canada Organic and USDA Organic. The maple leaf bottles and the 100 mL jug are not organic, and our cookies, stroopwafels, chocolates, snacks, tea and coffee are not certified organic.
Our online checkout ships to the ten Canadian provinces, and shipping is free on orders of $99 CAD or more. For an address in the territories or another country, email info@mapleterroir.com before you order and we will confirm whether we can ship there and what it costs.
If an item arrives damaged or defective, email info@mapleterroir.com within 7 days of delivery and we will replace it or refund it. Because these are food products, we cannot accept returns of opened items or returns for a change of mind.
