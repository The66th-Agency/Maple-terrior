What Kind of Maple Syrup Supplier We Are

Maple syrup suppliers come in three kinds: equipment sellers for people who make syrup, packers who sell pails and drums to kitchens and food makers, and companies that sell finished bottles for a shelf. We are the third kind. Everything we supply arrives packaged, labelled and ready to sell or hand out.

  • Stores and gift shops

    Leaf-shaped glass bottles in three sizes, two certified organic syrups and a shelf of maple cookies, stroopwafels, popcorn, tea and coffee.

  • Cafes, hotels and counters

    Stroopwafels come individually wrapped, 35 to a caddy, and the caddy opens into a display box for a counter or a till.

  • Offices and events

    A case or two for a staff kitchen or a conference can be ordered online today, with no minimum. For boxed gifts for staff and clients, see maple syrup corporate gifts.

  • What we do not supply

    Pails, drums or litre jugs of syrup for use as an ingredient. Our largest bottle is 250 mL. A kitchen that needs syrup by the pail is better served by a packer or a producers' cooperative.
