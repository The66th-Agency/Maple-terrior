Which Stocking Stuffer for Whom

Start with what the person eats or drinks in the morning. These pairings put two of our products together that get used the same way.

The coffee drinker

Our maple medium-dark roast is Arabica coffee roasted in Canada with maple syrup and natural maple flavour. Add a stroopwafel pack: set one on top of a hot mug for a minute and the maple filling softens.

The tea drinker

Maple Ceylon tea is black tea from Sri Lanka blended with our maple syrup and maple extract in Canada, 15 bags to a pouch. Pair it with a box of maple sugar and sea salt cookies, ten to a box and each one wrapped.

The baker

Organic maple sugar stands in for other sweeteners in a recipe, and it goes into coffee and over yogurt. With a jar of organic maple butter, that is $21.98 for two things a baker reaches for.

The chocolate lover

Our cocoa chocolate maple almonds are double roasted, coated in maple syrup, dipped in milk chocolate and dusted with cocoa. For dark chocolate, choose the cherry, cranberry or blueberry with maple syrup.

The kids

A bag of maple butter popcorn and a box of 15 mini stroopwafels, $13.98 together. The mini shortbread maple leaf cookies are made in a peanut-free facility, which helps for a classroom or a house with a peanut allergy.

A Canadian living away from home

The maple leaf bottle is shaped like the leaf on the flag, filled with Grade A Dark syrup. The 50 mL size at $7.99 leaves room in the stocking for a pack of stroopwafels and a box of shortbread.

Made in Canada, and We Say Where

Every stocking stuffer on this page is a Maple Terroir product made with the maple syrup we sell, and we can tell you where each one is made.

Where each one is made

Our stroopwafels are baked in Ontario. The maple blueberry cookies, maple sugar and sea salt cookies, maple popcorn and maple coffee are made in Vancouver.

Organic where the label says so

Our organic syrup is certified by Ecocert, Canada Organic and USDA Organic, and the organic maple sugar and maple butter are made from it. See our certifications.

Short ingredient lists

The first ingredient in our stroopwafels is flour, and the popcorn is Canadian-grown non-GMO corn with no preservatives or artificial colours. The syrup is syrup, with nothing added.
