01

Pure Maple Syrup, Set Firm

Maple Candy FAQ

Traditional maple candy is made from pure maple syrup and nothing else. The syrup is boiled to concentrate its sugars, stirred until it turns soft and opaque, then pressed into molds to set. Maple Terroir candy is made with pure Canadian maple syrup.

Pure maple syrup contains no gluten, so traditional maple candy made from it is naturally gluten-free. If you have a severe allergy, check the packaging for any shared-equipment notes before eating.

Kept in its wrapper in a cool, dry place, maple candy holds its texture for several weeks. Sealed and frozen, it keeps for months. Let it warm to room temperature before unwrapping so it does not draw moisture.

Maple candy is a soft, molded confection you eat as a treat. Maple sugar is the same maple boiled further and ground into a dry, granulated sugar you cook and bake with. Both start from pure maple syrup.
