How Maple Candy Is Made From Maple Syrup

Traditional maple candy has one ingredient. This is the method Maple from Canada publishes for it, and you need a cup of pure maple syrup, a candy thermometer and a wooden spoon.

  1. Boil to 113 °C

    That is 235 °F on a candy thermometer. Stir now and then so the syrup does not foam over the pot.

  2. Cool to 79 °C

    Take it off the heat and leave it alone, without stirring, until it falls to 175 °F, about two minutes.

  3. Stir 4 to 5 minutes

    Stir quickly with the wooden spoon until the syrup thickens and turns a lighter color.

  4. Mold and chill

    Pour it into the molds before it sets, spread it into the corners, and refrigerate for 10 minutes before you unmold it.

Maple cream starts the same way. Maple from Canada describes syrup heated to 112 °C, cooled quickly and churned into a spread instead of set in a mold, and our maple cream is syrup boiled to 112 °C and whipped smooth, with nothing added.
