What a Wedding Favour Has to Do

The favour is the one gift every guest receives, and it has to last past the last dance: into a coat pocket, a purse, a car or a carry-on bag. A favour that misses any of the four tests below tends to stay behind on the table when the room clears.

  1. 1

    Small enough to carry

    The 50 mL bottle holds a fifth of our 250 mL bottle, so it slips into a jacket pocket or a clutch with room to spare.

  2. 2

    Allowed on the flight home

    Canada's airport screening authority allows maple syrup in a container of 100 mL or less in a carry-on bag. Both of our favour sizes are within that limit.

  3. 3

    Something they will use

    A guest pours it on pancakes, stirs it into coffee or whisks it into a salad dressing, and the bottle is finished instead of stored in a drawer.

  4. 4

    It says Canada on its own

    The leaf shape and the Canada Grade A on the tag tell guests from out of the country where they were, without a label from you.

What Is in This Bottle

1Grade A Dark, Robust Taste

From the late harvest, when the syrup turns darker and the maple flavor stronger. Our guide to maple syrup grades compares Dark with the lighter grades.

2Nothing added

Pure maple syrup with no additives, chemicals, preservatives or coloring.

3From one farm

The syrup comes from one farm in Quebec, and the Lytton family has run Maple Terroir since 1978.

4A clear glass leaf

Clear glass shows the color of the syrup through the leaf. All three sizes hold the same syrup, so a 50 mL bottle tastes like the 250 mL one.

    Where the Bottles Go on the Day

    The same bottle works at five points across a wedding weekend. Decide where yours go before you count, because a welcome bag and a place setting can each need a bottle.

    • At each place setting

      On the napkin or the bread plate. Tie a small card with the guest's name to the neck and the bottle becomes the place card too.

    • On a favour table by the door

      A tray of bottles, as in the photo, that guests take on their way out. It suits open seating and a cocktail reception.

    • In welcome bags for out-of-town guests

      A 100 mL bottle and a pack of mini stroopwafels in the hotel bag give guests from away a first taste of Canada.

    • For parents and the wedding party

      The 100 mL bottle at the head table, or beside a thank-you card for the people who stood with you.

    • At the shower or the rehearsal dinner

      A case of 12 covers a small table, and the bottles match the favours guests will see again at the wedding.
