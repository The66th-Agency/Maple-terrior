Delivery Across Canada

Every gift set ships to any address in the ten provinces. Standard delivery takes 3 to 9 business days and costs $19.99, Express takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $39.99, and orders of $99 or more ship free. Vancouver buyers can choose free pickup at checkout.

For a gift that has to land by a date, count business days only and order a week or more ahead. Before the holidays, order in the first week of December.
