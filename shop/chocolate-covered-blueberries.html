Dark or Milk Chocolate Covered Blueberries

Shops sell chocolate covered blueberries in both, and the chocolate changes more than the colour. We coat ours in semi-sweet dark chocolate. The figures below come from Iowa State University Extension's guide to chocolate shelf life, read November 6, 2026.

Dark chocolate, as on oursMilk chocolate
What it isChocolate liquor, cocoa butter and sugar, with no milkAt least 10 percent chocolate liquor, plus milk solids, fat and sugar
TasteLess sweet; the higher the cacao, the darker and more bitterSweeter and creamier
Best qualityAt least 2 years for the chocolate itselfAbout 1 year, because milk fat goes rancid faster than cocoa butter

Shelf life here is for chocolate on its own. With fruit inside, go by the date and storage note on the box.

Are Chocolate Covered Blueberries Healthy?

They are chocolate, so read them as a treat. The panel on the box counts the whole 100 g box as one serving: 450 calories, 52 g of sugars and 15 g of saturated fat.

The berries and the dark chocolate add more than sugar. The same box has 8 g of fibre, 32 percent of the daily value, and 50 percent of the daily value for iron. Health Canada's guide to the percent daily value counts 15 percent or more as a lot, and names fibre and iron among the nutrients to get more of, and saturated fat and sugars among those to limit.

A quarter of the box comes to about 113 calories and 13 g of sugars.

How to Store Chocolate Covered Blueberries

Follow the storage note on the box first. The same Iowa State University Extension guide sets out how to keep chocolate at its best:

  1. 1

    Cool and dry. Room temperature, 18 to 21°C (65 to 70°F), with the humidity under 50 to 55 percent.

  2. 2

    Airtight. Once the box is open, move what is left to a sealed container. Cocoa butter takes on the smell of whatever sits near it, and air dulls the flavour.

  3. 3

    Dark. A pantry or cupboard, away from a sunny counter.

  4. 4

    Fridge only in real heat. Chocolate taken out of the fridge gathers moisture. If you must chill it, wrap it tightly.

A grey, dusty surface is bloom, from heat or damp. The guide says bloom changes only the look: the taste is the same and the chocolate is safe to eat.
