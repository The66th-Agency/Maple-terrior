What Is in the Bottle

Each leaf bottle holds Grade A Dark, Robust Taste maple syrup from the late harvest, the end of the sugaring season, when the syrup turns darker and the maple flavor stronger. It is pure maple syrup with no additives, preservatives or coloring.

The syrup comes from one family farm in Quebec, and the Lytton family has run Maple Terroir since 1978. The three sizes are filled with the same syrup, so a 50 mL bottle tastes like the 250 mL one.

The bottle is clear glass, so the syrup shows its color through the leaf. Our guide to maple syrup grades explains how Dark compares with the lighter grades. For a lighter, early-harvest syrup, our organic 250 mL Golden is $14.99 in a round bottle.

Who Buys a Maple Leaf Bottle

The leaf bottle says where it comes from before anyone opens it, so it works as a gift as well as a pantry bottle. Here are five ways to use one, and the size that suits each.

A souvenir for the flight

Canada's airport screening authority, CATSA, allows maple syrup in a carry-on in containers of 100 mL or less, all inside one clear 1 L bag. The 50 mL and 100 mL bottles qualify, and the 250 mL goes in a checked bag. More picks for the trip are on our Canadian souvenirs page.

A gift from home

For family or friends who have moved away, the 250 mL leaf bottle is the one to send. Pair it with our stroopwafels and shortbread from our Canadian stocking stuffers, or choose a boxed set from our Canadian gift baskets.

A place card for every guest

At a wedding, a reunion or a conference dinner, a 50 mL bottle sits at each setting. A 48-pack is $287.52, or $5.99 a bottle, and you can order more than one pack.

A thank-you for clients and staff

A 100 mL bottle for each person on a team, or a 250 mL for each client, at 10 to 25 percent off by the pack. Our corporate gift page covers orders sent to many addresses.

Breakfast at home

The robust taste holds up over pancakes, waffles, oatmeal and roasted vegetables. For syrup by the jug rather than the leaf, see our maple syrup collection.
