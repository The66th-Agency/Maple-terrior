How to Use Maple Sugar

1 cup white sugar
= 1 cup maple sugar

Swap it in equal amounts in recipes that call for refined sugar, so there is nothing to convert.

In coffee and tea
Stir it in by the spoon, the way you would white sugar.
Over breakfast
Sprinkle it over crepes, yogurt and ice cream.
In the oven and on the grill
Bake it into cookies and cakes, or mix it into a rub for meat and vegetables. Our guide to maple sugar has a rub, a cocktail syrup and tips on storing it.

Questions About Maple Sugar

Maple sugar is maple syrup cooked past the syrup stage and stirred until it dries into granules. Ours is made from 100 percent organic pure maple syrup, and the ingredient list on the box reads organic maple syrup and nothing else.

Yes. Maple from Canada says maple sugar replaces refined sugar in equal quantity, so a recipe that calls for a cup of white sugar takes a cup of maple sugar.

It tastes of maple, in a dry form you can measure. Maple from Canada describes its flavor as distinctive yet delicate and suggests it for coffee and tea as well as for sweet and savory dishes.
