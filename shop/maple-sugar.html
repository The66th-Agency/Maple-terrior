How to Use Maple Sugar

Anywhere you reach for white or brown sugar, you can reach for this instead.

Swap It One for One

Use maple sugar in place of white or brown sugar at a one-to-one ratio in most recipes. No conversion, just a deeper, maple-led sweetness.

Stir Into Coffee and Tea

It dissolves hot or cold, so it sweetens coffee, tea, and cocktails with maple flavor and none of the stickiness of pouring syrup.

Finish and Bake

Sprinkle it over crepes, yogurt, oatmeal, and ice cream, or bake it into cookies, glazes, and rubs where you want maple to lead.

Maple Sugar vs Maple Syrup

Both start as maple sap. The syrup is boiled until it pours. The sugar is boiled further, until it dries and granulates.

Maple Syrup

Pourable and liquid. Reach for it to top pancakes and yogurt, to glaze, or to sweeten anything you want to drizzle.

Maple Sugar

Dry and granulated. Reach for it to measure, sprinkle, and bake, anywhere a recipe calls for sugar by the cup or spoon.
