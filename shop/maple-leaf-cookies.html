What Is in the Box

Our Maple Leaf Mini Shortbread Cookies are small, crisp maple leaves in a bilingual box. Here is what the box tells you.

1Made with pure maple syrup

Pure maple syrup is in the dough with the sugar and brown sugar, and a natural maple flavour rounds it out. The box lists no preservatives, artificial flavours or colouring.

2Bite-size maple leaves

Each cookie is a small maple leaf pressed with veins, sized to eat in one or two bites, so a box shares easily around a table.

3English and French

The box reads Sablé mini, Sirop d’érable under the English name, so it works as a Canadian gift in either language.

4A family business since 1978

Printed along the bottom of every box. The Lytton family has run Maple Terroir since 1978, and our story page tells how it started.
