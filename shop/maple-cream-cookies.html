How Ours Compares With the Classic

The maple leaf cream cookie is a Canadian classic, and many bakeries and grocery brands make their own. They share the leaf shape and the cream centre, so three lines on the box tell them apart. Here is what to read, and what ours says.

Read this on the boxWhy it mattersOur Maple Syrup Cream Cookies
The ingredient listMaple syrup and maple flavour are different ingredients. Look for maple syrup by name if that is what you want.Made with pure maple syrup, printed on the front
Trans fat in the nutrition tableThe Nutrition Facts table on Canadian food shows trans fat in grams, next to saturated fat.0 g per 2 cookies
How many, and how they are packedA count tells you how far a box goes. Wrapped cookies suit lunch bags; loose cookies suit a plate.18 to a box from 2027, not individually wrapped

Baking your own while ours are away? Our guide to the best maple syrup for baking says which grade to use and why the Dark grade, the strongest maple flavour of the grades we sell, carries through to the finished cookie.
