What the Organic Label Tells You

“100 percent pure” describes what is in the bottle. “Organic” describes how the syrup was produced, and an independent certifier checks it. In Canada, a label making an organic claim must meet the federal organic rules when the product is sold between provinces or imported. Under the Canadian organic standard for maple, these three rules set organic syrup apart.

Plant-based defoamers only

Boiling sap foams. Organic producers may use only unaltered plant-based defoamers, such as organic vegetable oil, and no synthetic ones (clause 7.2.12.5).

Smaller tap holes

Organic producers drill 5/16 inch tap holes and limit how many taps each tree carries, to protect the tree (clauses 7.2.10.1 and 7.2.10.2).

A mix of trees

Trees other than sugar maple should make up at least 15 percent of the wood in the sugar bush, and producers below that must encourage them to grow (clause 7.2.9.1).
