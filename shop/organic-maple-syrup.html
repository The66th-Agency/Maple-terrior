Our Three Organic Certifications

Our organic syrups are certified organic by Ecocert, Canada Organic and USDA Organic.

Ecocert

Ecocert certifies our organic syrups.

Canada Organic

Administered by the Canadian Food Inspection Agency. Required to make any organic claim on Canadian retail shelves. The standard covers permitted substances, soil management, and processing facility conditions.

USDA Organic

The National Organic Program standard.

The certification covers our two organic syrups only. The Pure Maple Syrup Maple Leaf Bottle is not organic, and the certification does not cover our stroopwafels, chocolates or cookies.
