Why the dates move.

The University of Maine Cooperative Extension names temperature, air and time as the three things that wear stored syrup down. Each answer above is about one of them.

The seal

A sealed bottle of pure maple syrup keeps for a long time. Once you open it, air reaches the surface, and over months mold can form on top, which is why opened syrup goes in the fridge.

The container

Plastic lets a little air through. Purdue Extension says to finish syrup kept in plastic within three to six months, and the University of Maine found syrup in plastic can darken by a full grade in three months.

The freezer

Pure maple syrup is so high in sugar that it does not freeze solid. Purdue Extension suggests a freezer-safe glass jar with an inch of space at the top, sealed tightly.

The mold

If mold forms, throw the syrup out. The University of Maine Cooperative Extension warns that mold toxins can survive boiling, so skimming it off and reboiling does not make the syrup safe.

Storage questions.

How long does maple syrup last once opened?

In the fridge, 6 to 12 months in glass and 3 to 6 months in plastic. Purdue Extension notes the color and flavor start to change within six to eight months of opening.

Does unopened maple syrup go bad?

Rarely. Maple from Canada says a sealed glass bottle kept somewhere cool and dry can last several years, and a sealed plastic bottle up to two.

Do you have to refrigerate maple syrup?

Once it is open, yes. Pure maple syrup has no preservatives, and both Purdue Extension and the University of Maine advise keeping an opened bottle in the fridge to prevent mold.

Can you freeze maple syrup?

Yes. It stays pourable because of its sugar content, and Maple from Canada says it keeps in the freezer indefinitely. Leave an inch of space at the top of the jar.
