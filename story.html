Three Generations of One Maple Family.

Wayne Lytton, born in Nanaimo, B.C., started Maple Terroir with his wife Kaori in 1978. Their son Shawn runs the company today, and Gavin Lytton packs and ships the online orders from Vancouver.

When you order from mapleterroir.com, the order goes to that family, and they are the ones who pack it.

From One Farm in Quebec
to Your Door

We buy all of our syrup from one farm, so every bottle we sell has the same origin, and we can tell you exactly where it was made.

  1. One family farm in Quebec's Appalachian Mountains

    Every drop of syrup we sell comes from this one farm. Sap from early in the season makes our organic Golden syrup, and sap from late in the season makes the Dark syrup in our maple leaf bottles, which is why the two taste so different.

  2. Our Vancouver location

    We pack and ship every online order from 9291 Shaughnessy Street in Vancouver, and you can pick yours up there for free.

  3. Your door, in any Canadian province

    Standard delivery takes 3 to 9 business days for $19.99, Express takes 1 to 2 business days for $39.99, and orders of $99 CAD or more ship free.

What Is in Our
Organic Syrup

Our Organic Pure Maple Syrup in the 250 mL bottle is the one we suggest if you are trying us for the first time. Here is what is in it.

1From one farm

Single origin, from the farm in Quebec's Appalachian Mountains, and made from sap collected early in the season.

2Nothing added

No additives, chemicals, preservatives or coloring, so maple syrup is all you pour.

3Three organic certifications

Ecocert, Canada Organic and USDA Organic certify this syrup, and our certifications page explains what each one checks. Our maple leaf bottles are not organic.

4Grade A Golden, Delicate Taste

A light syrup for drizzling and desserts. For cooking and baking, the maple leaf bottle holds our Dark, Robust syrup.
