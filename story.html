A family maple business since 1978.

How Maple Terroir started.

Wayne Lytton, born in Nanaimo, B.C., started Maple Terroir with his wife Kaori in 1978. The maple syrup we sell comes from one farm in the Appalachian Mountains of Quebec.

Their son Shawn runs the company today as CEO, and Gavin Lytton packs and ships the online orders from Vancouver.
