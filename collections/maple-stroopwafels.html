Maple Stroopwafel FAQ

What is in a Maple Terroir stroopwafel?

Canadian-grown flour, Canadian eggs, butter, cane sugar, palm oil, sea salt and pure maple syrup, with no preservatives or artificial flavoring, according to the product page. Every stroopwafel in our caddies is individually wrapped.

Do Maple Terroir stroopwafels use real maple or artificial maple flavor?

Real maple. The filling is sweetened with pure maple syrup, and the product pages list no artificial flavoring.

Are the stroopwafels gluten-free?

No. The stroopwafels are made with Canadian wheat flour and are not gluten-free. Our Pure Maple Syrup Butter Popcorn is made with gluten-free ingredients, according to its product page. Check the label if you have a severe allergy.

Can I order maple stroopwafels in bulk or case quantities for wholesale?

Yes. The Pure Maple Syrup Stroopwafels caddy is 10 percent off by the 8 on its product page. For case pricing, see the wholesale page or email info@mapleterroir.com. For gifts to clients or staff, see our corporate gifts page.

Are the stroopwafels organic?

No. The stroopwafels are not certified organic. They are sweetened with pure maple syrup.
