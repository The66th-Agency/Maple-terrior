How to read a maple syrup label.

Pure maple syrup is sorted by color and flavor, not by quality. The old "Grade B" label has been retired, and every pure syrup is now Grade A with a color modifier. The deep, full-bodied syrup you remember as Grade B maple syrup is now sold as Grade A Dark Robust or Grade A Very Dark. Same syrup, clearer name.

Every Maple Terroir syrup is Grade A. Our organic syrups are Golden, the delicate early-season grade, and the maple leaf bottle is Dark, the robust late-harvest grade.

Premium Maple Syrup FAQ

Where does this maple syrup come from?

Every bottle comes from one family farm in Quebec's Appalachian Mountains. Our organic syrups are certified organic by Ecocert, Canada Organic and USDA Organic.

What does single-origin mean for maple syrup?

Single-origin means the syrup in every bottle comes from one farm. Ours is a family farm.

Is your maple syrup certified organic?

Our organic syrups are. The Organic Pure Maple Syrup and the Organic First Tap Nouveau are certified organic by Ecocert, Canada Organic and USDA Organic. The Pure Maple Syrup Maple Leaf Bottle is not organic, and the certification does not cover our stroopwafels, cookies, chocolates or other treats.

What grade of maple syrup do you sell?

All of our syrup is Canada Grade A. The Organic Pure Maple Syrup and the Organic First Tap Nouveau are Golden, with a delicate taste. The Pure Maple Syrup Maple Leaf Bottle is Dark, with a robust taste. Each product page lists its grade.

Can I order premium maple syrup wholesale or in bulk?

Yes. Every syrup is cheaper in quantity on its product page: 10 percent off 12 bottles, 15 percent off 24, 20 percent off 36 and 25 percent off 48. For larger orders, see the maple syrup wholesale page or email info@mapleterroir.com.
