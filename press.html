A Canadian maple family, ready to talk terroir.

Maple Terroir is a third-generation family business in Vancouver. We sell maple syrup from one farm, including organic syrup certified by Ecocert, Canada Organic and USDA Organic. Wayne and Kaori Lytton started the company in 1978, and their son Shawn runs it today as CEO. We welcome interview requests, product features and collaborations.

Quick facts for your story.

Founded
1978, by Wayne Lytton and his wife Kaori. Their son Shawn runs the company as CEO, and three generations of the Lytton family work in the business.
Headquarters
9291 Shaughnessy Street, Vancouver, BC V6P 6P4. Online orders ship from here, and local pickup is free.
Origin
Single-origin syrup sourced from one farm in the Appalachian Mountains of Quebec.
Certifications
Ecocert, Canada Organic and USDA Organic, for our organic maple syrup. The maple leaf bottles and jug are not organic.
Signature Product
Maple-leaf-shaped glass bottles. Also stroopwafels, chocolates, cookies, tea and coffee, and gift sets made with our maple syrup.
Harvests
Early-season sap for the organic syrup and First Tap Nouveau (Canada Grade A Golden, Delicate Taste). Later-season sap for the maple leaf bottles and jug (Canada Grade A Dark, Robust Taste).
Distribution
Online at mapleterroir.com, shipping to the ten Canadian provinces. In Canada through Costco, Amazon, Walmart, Save-On-Foods, Shoppers Drug Mart, HomeSense, Urban Fare, Nesters Market and CANEX. Exported to Japan, South Korea, China and Taiwan.
