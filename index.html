Frequently Asked Questions

Where does your maple syrup come from?

Every bottle of our maple syrup comes from one family farm in Quebec's Appalachian Mountains, and we ship it to you from our Vancouver location. We sell two grades from that farm: Golden, from early in the season, and Dark, from late in the season.

Do you offer any discounts for bulk purchases?

Yes. Many of our products come in multi-packs that cost less per item, and each product page lists its pack sizes and prices. Our 250 mL syrup bottles, for example, come in packs of 12, 24, 36 and 48 at 10, 15, 20 and 25 percent off: a bottle of our organic syrup is $14.99 on its own and $11.25 each in a pack of 48. For larger orders, see our wholesale page.

How quickly will my order ship?

We pack and ship orders from our Vancouver location within 1 to 3 business days. Standard delivery then takes 3 to 9 business days and costs $19.99, or nothing on orders of $99 CAD or more. Express takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $39.99.

What is your return policy?

If an item arrives damaged or defective, email info@mapleterroir.com or call +1 (604) 324-6556 within 7 days of delivery, and we will replace it or refund it. Because we sell food, we cannot accept returns of opened items or returns for a change of mind.

Where can I buy Maple Terroir maple syrup in Canada?

Order on this site and we ship to every Canadian province from our Vancouver location, with free shipping on orders of $99 CAD or more. For delivery times to your city, see our guides for Vancouver, Toronto, Montreal, Calgary, Ottawa, Edmonton and Winnipeg.

Are your products suitable for gifts?

Yes. We sell gift sets and home sets from $75 to $240, and our First Tap Nouveau syrup comes in its own gift box. Each set's page lists what is inside. See the gift sets and home sets.

How can I contact customer service?

Email info@mapleterroir.com or call +1 (604) 324-6556, Monday to Friday, 10 AM to 3 PM Pacific time, except statutory holidays. You can also reach us through our contact page.

Do you ship internationally?

We ship online orders to the ten Canadian provinces only, not to Yukon, the Northwest Territories, Nunavut or other countries. For one of those addresses, email info@mapleterroir.com before you order, and we will tell you whether we can send it and what it would cost.

How should I store my maple syrup once it is opened?

Keep unopened bottles in a cool, dry cupboard. Once a bottle is open, keep it in the fridge and use it within 6 months. If sugar crystals form, warm the bottle gently in hot water until the syrup is smooth again.

What is the difference between Golden and Dark maple syrup?

In Canada, Grade A maple syrup is sorted by color into four classes, each named for its taste, and we sell two of them. Our organic syrups are Golden, Delicate Taste: they come from early in the season and have a light flavor for drizzling and desserts. Our maple leaf bottles are Dark, Robust Taste: they come from late in the season and have a stronger maple flavor for cooking and baking.

Are your products certified organic?

Our organic maple syrups, the organic 250 mL bottle and First Tap Nouveau, are certified organic by Ecocert, Canada Organic and USDA Organic, and they are the only products we sell with that certification. Our maple leaf bottles and our syrup jug are not certified organic. Each product page lists its ingredients.

Do you sell wholesale?

Yes. We supply grocers, gourmet food halls, gift shops and corporate gifting programs, and we also sell our syrup in Japan, South Korea, China and Taiwan. Our minimum wholesale order is one full case. See our wholesale page for how to order.
