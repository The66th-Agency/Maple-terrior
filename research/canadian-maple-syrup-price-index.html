Canadian Maple Syrup Price Index, Fall 2026

The median maple syrup price in Canada was $2.76 per 100 mL on October 23, 2026, across 34 bottles of pure maple syrup from 16 brands, read from 10 grocery and brand websites. Grocery brands and store brands had medians of $2.04 and $2.10, smaller maple brands selling from their own websites $3.40, and the two table syrups we checked cost $0.50 and $0.57.

Key Findings

Every figure is a price per 100 mL in Canadian dollars, before tax and shipping, read from the seller's website on October 23, 2026. Each one compares bottles of 100 percent pure maple syrup unless it says otherwise. Maple Terroir sells maple syrup, so we left our own bottles out of this index.

$2.76

The median price per 100 mL of 34 bottles of pure maple syrup sold online in Canada.

$2.10

The median for store brands: Kirkland Signature, Our Finest, Compliments and Panache, 10 bottles from 375 mL to 1 L. Grocery brands were close, at $2.04.

61% more

What smaller maple brands charged on their own websites against store brands, compared at 1 L and over: $2.90 against $1.80 per 100 mL.

2.1 times

The price per 100 mL of a 250 to 375 mL bottle against a bottle of 1 L or more: a median of $4.20 against $2.00.

Over 5 times

The median price of pure maple syrup against the two table syrups we checked: $2.76 against $0.54 per 100 mL.

1.4 to 1.8 times

What a 250 mL leaf-shaped glass bottle cost against the plain 250 mL bottle of the same syrup from the same maker, at three makers.

Price by Type of Brand

Store brands and grocery brands were close: a median of $2.10 per 100 mL for store brands and $2.04 for grocery brands. Smaller maple brands selling from their own websites had a median of $3.40, 62 percent above store brands.

The groups hold different sizes: 7 of the 15 smaller-brand bottles in the basket are 250 mL, against none of the 10 store brand bottles. Compared at 1 L and over, store brands had a median of $1.80 per 100 mL, grocery brands $1.70 and the smaller brands $2.90, so the gap at the same size is 61 percent.

Price by Bottle Size

In this basket, bigger bottles cost less per 100 mL. Bottles of 1 L and over had a median of $2.00 per 100 mL, while 250 to 375 mL bottles had a median of $4.20, 2.1 times as much.

The cheapest pure maple syrup in the basket was a 4 L jug of Bernard Canada Grade A Amber at Costco, at $1.57 per 100 mL, out of stock online that day. At 1 L, the cheapest were Our Finest Organic and Old Fashioned Maple Crest Dark at Walmart, both at $1.70.

Pure Maple Syrup and Table Syrup

Pure maple syrup cost more than five times as much as table syrup per 100 mL: a median of $2.76 against $0.54. Even the cheapest pure syrup in the basket, at $1.57, cost 2.8 times the more expensive of the two table syrups.

The two table syrups were Compliments Syrup Original at Voilà, $4.29 for 750 mL, and Pearl Milling Company Original Syrup at Costco, $18.99 for 3.78 L. Neither has the word maple in its name. Our guide to pure maple syrup and table syrup explains how to tell them apart on the label.

The Same Bottle at Two Sellers

The same 250 mL bottle of Dutchman's Gold maple syrup cost $15.99 on walmart.ca and $6.79 on the maker's own website, 2.4 times as much. The maker's price was a sale price, down from $7.99, and the walmart.ca price was still twice that regular price.

Organic Syrup

Where one brand sold the same size with and without organic certification, organic cost 11 percent more: Kirkland Signature Organic was $19.99 for 1 L at Costco, against $17.99 for Kirkland Signature, both out of stock online that day. That is the one like-for-like pair in this edition.

Organic did not always cost more. Our Finest Organic at Walmart, $16.97 for 1 L, tied for the cheapest 1 L bottle in the basket at $1.70 per 100 mL. Our guide to organic and conventional maple syrup covers what the certification checks.

How We Did the Research

We read the price of 38 bottles on October 23, 2026: 34 bottles of pure maple syrup from 16 brands, 2 table syrups, and 2 walmart.ca listings of bottles we also read on the maker's website. Every price is in Canadian dollars, before tax and shipping, as the seller's website showed it that day.

The basket is fixed: each edition reads the same bottles from the same sellers, so any change between editions is a change in price. The January 2027 edition will report the change on every bottle read both times.

Price per 100 mL is the price divided by the bottle size in mL, times 100. A median is the middle value of a group, so one bottle at either extreme moves it little. A sale price counts as the price that day, with the regular price shown beside it; 4 bottles were on sale.

The basket holds bottles of 100 percent pure maple syrup from 250 mL to 4 L. We left out flavored and infused syrups, multi-bottle cases, gift sets and syrup from other trees.

We read the websites of Costco, Walmart and Voilà, and the online shops of seven maple brands. Costco prices are its online prices; all four Costco bottles were out of stock online that day, and the table notes it. Walmart prices come from one page of its search results on walmart.ca. Voilà prices are the ones shown before you choose a delivery address.

We could not read prices on the websites of Loblaw stores, such as No Frills and Real Canadian Superstore, or on Metro, Save-On-Foods or London Drugs that day. Their store brands, among them President's Choice, No Name, Irresistibles, Selection and Western Family, are missing from this edition. We left out Amazon, where many sellers list the same bottle at different prices.

Sizes and descriptions are as each seller lists them. We did not test any syrup, so pure and organic are the words on each label. The table syrup group has 2 bottles, so treat its median as a small sample.

Maple Terroir sells maple syrup, so we have a stake in these prices, and we left our own bottles out of this index.

What This Means When You Buy

Here is how we read these numbers, as a maple syrup company. In this basket, a bottle of 1 L or more cost a median of $2.00 per 100 mL, under half the $4.20 of a 250 to 375 mL bottle.

  • Buy the largest bottle you will finish. Our guide to how long maple syrup keeps covers storing an open bottle.
  • Compare the unit price. The same 250 mL bottle of Dutchman's Gold cost $6.79 from the maker and $15.99 on walmart.ca.
  • Buy the leaf bottle as a gift. At 250 mL it cost 1.4 to 1.8 times the plain bottle of the same syrup.
  • Read the name on the label. Every pure syrup in this edition had maple in its name; neither table syrup did.
