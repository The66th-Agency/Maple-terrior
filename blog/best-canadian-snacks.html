The Best Canadian Snacks, Ranked for 2026

The Short Answer

The best Canadian snack to buy is Maple Terroir stroopwafels, which score 10 out of 14: they are made in Canada, mainly from Canadian ingredients, with a pure maple syrup filling. Ketchup chips come second with 9, and they are the snack Canadians name most, ahead of All Dressed chips, Coffee Crisp and Jos. Louis, going by nine Canadian snack lists.

Maple Terroir stroopwafels: made in Canada, mainly from Canadian ingredients, with a pure maple syrup filling, four to a box for $8.99. 10 out of 14.

01

Maple Syrup Stroopwafels

Maple Terroir. $8.99 for a box of four, made in Canada.

10/ 14

A stroopwafel is two thin waffle layers pressed around a caramel filling. Maple Terroir fills its stroopwafels with maple syrup from one farm in Quebec, and its product page says they are made mainly from Canadian ingredients: Canadian-grown flour first, then Canadian eggs and sea salt, with no margarine or glucose-fructose. Set one on top of a hot cup of tea or coffee for a minute and the filling softens.

Maple Terroir stroopwafels were supplied to the TED Conference in Vancouver in 2024, 2025 and 2026. For a smaller bite, the mini stroopwafels come 15 to a box at $8.99.

02

Ketchup Chips

Lay's (PepsiCo Canada) and Old Dutch. Named by 5 of the 9 lists, more than any other snack.

9/ 14

Ketchup chips are the snack Canadians name first: five of the nine lists we read include them, more than any other snack. The Canadian Encyclopedia says Hostess sold most of them in the 1970s and 1980s, and that nobody knows for certain who made them first.

Lay's says its ketchup chips are "Made in Canada from domestic and imported ingredients." Old Dutch, a family-owned company that started in Winnipeg in 1954, says it makes its chips for the Canadian market in four facilities across Canada. Both are grocery store chips; neither maker sells online.

The Snacks Canadians Name Most

We read the nine Canadian snack lists on the first page of Google Canada for "canadian snacks" and counted how many named each snack. Ketchup chips appear on five of the nine. Something made with maple appears on five of the nine too, from maple cream cookies to maple taffy, though no single maple snack reaches more than two.

The lists: getlost.blog, "Top Canadian Snacks"; Narcity, "I Tried 7 Canadian Snacks For The First Time"; Narcity, "7 Canadian snacks I didn't know existed"; Reformatt, "15 Typical Canadian Snacks and Drinks"; TasteAtlas, "6 Best Snacks in Canada"; Canadian Food Focus, "The Snack Story"; PSS Removals, "Popular Canadian Foods"; Klondike Kettle Corn, "The Best Canadian Snacks to Try Right Now"; Merry About Town, "Yummy Canadian Products". Brands are merged where a list names the same snack (Lay's and Old Dutch ketchup chips count as ketchup chips).

How We Scored Them

We took the packaged snacks the nine lists name most often, added three Canadian-made snacks the lists missed (Swedish Berries, Purdys Hedgehogs and Caramilk) and three Maple Terroir snacks, and checked each one on its maker's website on November 6, 2026. Dishes and bakery treats such as poutine and butter tarts are covered separately below, because you eat them where they are made.

How many of the nine lists name a snack counts for up to 4 points, because it is the plainest record of which snacks Canadians call their own, and it also orders snacks with the same score. We did not run a taste test, so taste is not scored. Every score comes from what a maker or a named source publishes, which is what you can check yourself.

Canadians name it
1 point for each of the nine Canadian snack lists that names it, up to 4.
Made with pure maple
2 points if pure maple syrup or pure maple sugar is an ingredient, per the maker, 0 if it is not.
Made in Canada
2 points if the maker says it is made in Canada, 1 if only a retailer or an independent source says so, 0 if we found no statement.
Canadian ingredients
2 points if the maker says it is made mainly from Canadian ingredients, 1 if the maker names one Canadian-grown ingredient or says "domestic and imported", or an independent source names a Canadian ingredient, 0 if not stated.
Travels well
2 points for a shelf-stable snack that comes in a box, is wrapped one by one or cannot crush, such as gummies; 1 for crisp snacks in a soft bag, which can crush, and for chocolate or soft cake, which can melt or squash; 0 if it needs a fridge.
Buy from the maker
2 points if the maker sells it online in Canada, 1 if it is sold in grocery stores, 0 if we found neither.

Canadian Treats to Eat in Canada

Four of the most-named treats are fresh foods rather than packaged snacks, so they are not scored above. They are worth eating while you are here, or baking at home.

Butter tarts

Named by 4 lists. Small pastry tarts with a sweet, buttery filling. The Canadian Encyclopedia says the first published recipe appeared in 1900, in a cookbook from the Women's Auxiliary of the Royal Victoria Hospital.

Poutine

Named by 4 lists. Fries, cheese curds and gravy. The Canadian Encyclopedia says the combination first appeared in rural snack bars in the late 1950s. It is a hot dish, so it is a snack to eat in Canada rather than pack.

BeaverTails

Named by 3 lists. Fried pastries made from a Hooker family recipe. Pam and Grant Hooker first sold them at the Killaloe Craft and Community Fair in 1978, according to the company's own history, and today they are made fresh in BeaverTails shops.

Nanaimo bars

Named by 2 lists. A no-bake chocolate square. The Canadian Encyclopedia dates the first version to a 1952 Nanaimo Hospital cookbook; the City of Nanaimo calls its origin "elusive."

Choosing Canadian Snacks to Send or Bring Home

Pick snacks that are boxed or wrapped one by one, and shelf-stable. Boxed stroopwafels, wrapped cookies and gummies hold their shape in a packed bag; chips, popcorn and Cheezies come in soft bags that can crush, and chocolate bars and Jos. Louis cakes melt or squash in the heat. If the label matters to the person, check that the maker says the snack is made in Canada: of the nine other snacks here, eight carry that claim from the maker or an independent source.

Ketchup chips. Five of the nine Canadian snack lists on the first page of Google Canada name them, more than any other snack. All Dressed chips, Coffee Crisp, Jos. Louis, butter tarts and poutine follow with four lists each.
