Maple Glazed Salmon in 25 Minutes

Five pantry ingredients make the glaze: maple syrup, soy sauce, Dijon mustard, garlic and black pepper. Brush it on, bake, brush again, and dinner is on the table in under half an hour.

By Maple Terroir October 19, 2026 4 min read
Prep10 min
Bake12 to 15 min
Serves4
Oven400°F (200°C)

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets, about 170 g (6 oz) each, skin on or off
  • ¼ cup (60 mL) pure maple syrup
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper, plus salt to taste
  • 1 green onion, thinly sliced, to finish
  • Lemon wedges, to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking tray with parchment.
  2. Whisk the maple syrup, soy sauce, Dijon, garlic and pepper in a small bowl. Set a third of it aside in a second bowl for the end.
  3. Pat the salmon dry, set it on the tray skin side down, season lightly with salt and brush the tops with the glaze.
  4. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, brushing once more halfway through. Health Canada's safe internal temperature for fish is 70°C (158°F); check the thickest fillet with a thermometer.
  5. Brush on the glaze you set aside, scatter the green onion and serve with lemon.

Which Maple Syrup to Use

Use pure maple syrup, not a pancake syrup made from corn syrup and flavoring, which turns thin and overly sweet in the oven. A darker, stronger-tasting syrup holds its own against soy sauce and mustard, and a golden one gives a lighter, sweeter glaze. Our guide to maple syrup grades explains the difference.

Keep the glaze off the heat until the salmon goes in. Maple syrup is mostly sugar, so it browns fast, and the dark edges on the fillets are the glaze caramelizing. If the edges darken before the center is done, lay a sheet of foil loosely over the tray for the last few minutes.

Three Ways to Change It

On the grill. Cook the fillets skin side down over medium heat with the lid closed, and brush the glaze on only for the last few minutes so the sugar does not burn on the grates.

With heat. Add a teaspoon of sriracha or a pinch of chili flakes to the glaze.

With ginger. Grate a teaspoon of fresh ginger into the glaze along with the garlic.
