Maple Terroir at TED: Our Stroopwafels at TED2024, TED2025 and TED2026

By Maple Terroir 4 min read
Published January 30, 2025 Last updated October 29, 2026

TED2026 in Vancouver: an attendee opens one of our Pure Maple Syrup Stroopwafels beside TED's card thanking its in-kind partners. Photo: Felicia Chang for TED.

TED served Maple Terroir stroopwafels to the people at its conference in Vancouver three years in a row: TED2024, TED2025 and TED2026. We took part as an in-kind partner, which means we gave the stroopwafels and TED set them out for its attendees between talks. This page tells that story with TED's own facts and with photos from the conference.

  • The customer: TED, the organization behind TED Talks, at its yearly TED Conference.
  • What we gave: individually wrapped maple stroopwafels, made in Canada with Canadian flour, butter and eggs and a filling made with pure maple syrup.
  • When: April 2024, April 2025 and April 2026, in Vancouver.
  • The result: three TED Conferences in a row as an in-kind partner, the last of them TED's final conference in Vancouver.

What TED needed

The TED Conference runs for five days, and TED says more than a thousand people attend. TED2024 ran from April 15 to 19, 2024, at the Vancouver Convention Centre, under the theme "The Brave and the Brilliant". It marked 40 years since the first TED conference, held in 1984. BizBash reported that TED2024 drew about 1,800 attendees, who watched the talks in a 1,200-seat theater built inside the venue.

That many people, over five days, need food they can pick up between sessions and carry back to their seats. TED fills part of that with in-kind partners: companies that give their products for the event. In TED's own words, these partners help by "donating their goods and services."

Why a Maple Terroir stroopwafel fit

The same TED post says each in-kind partner is carefully selected, for the quality of its product and for values that match TED's. Here is what we brought.

A stroopwafel is two thin waffle layers pressed together with a syrup filling. Ours have pure maple syrup in the filling and are made in Canada with Canadian flour, butter and eggs. Each 34-gram stroopwafel comes in its own wrapper, so an attendee can take one from a shelf, put it in a pocket and eat it in the next break. Maple Terroir has been a family business since 1978, when Wayne and Kaori Lytton started it, and our office is in Vancouver, so TED was a conference in our own city.

What we did at each conference

TED2024: The Brave and the Brilliant

At TED2024 we gave our stroopwafels for the attendees. The two photos below show them in attendees' hands, both wearing TED2024 lanyards: a Strawberry Maple stroopwafel picked from the snack wall, and a Blueberry Maple stroopwafel opened outside the venue, under the TED sign.

TED2025: Humanity Reimagined

TED2025 ran from April 7 to 11, 2025, in Vancouver, under the theme "Humanity Reimagined". We returned as an in-kind partner and gave stroopwafels for the attendees again. The photos on this page come from 2024 and 2026.

TED2026: All of Us

TED2026 ran from April 13 to 17, 2026, under the theme "All of Us". It was TED's final year in Vancouver, after 12 years in the city, before the conference moves to San Diego. We were an in-kind partner for the third year in a row.

TED's photographer, Felicia Chang, took photos of the in-kind partners' products for TED. In the photo at the top of this page, an attendee opens one of our Pure Maple Syrup Stroopwafels. In the photo below, an open box of our Chocolate Maple Stroopwafels sits on the bottom shelf of the partner snack wall, at the far left.

The results

We were an in-kind partner at three TED Conferences in a row, from 2024 to 2026, including TED's last conference in Vancouver. Each year TED set our stroopwafels out for its attendees, and at TED2024 that was about 1,800 people. We did not count how many stroopwafels went to TED in any year, so this page gives no number.

For a company planning an event, the photos show what this looks like on the day: a wrapped stroopwafel on a shelf, picked up between talks and opened on the spot.
