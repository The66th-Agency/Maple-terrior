Christmas Gifts for Coworkers: 12 Picks for 2026

The best Christmas gifts for coworkers are small, get used up and suit any desk: a maple leaf bottle of syrup at $7.99, a pack of stroopwafels, a box of tea or coffee. Twelve picks below, sorted by who they suit and what they cost, from a Secret Santa limit to a gift from the whole team.

Published October 25, 2026 Last updated October 25, 2026

A good Christmas gift for a coworker costs under $25, gets eaten or used within a few weeks, and needs no guessing about taste or size. Food fits all three, which is why most of this list is something to eat or drink at a desk.

We sell maple products, and eight of the twelve picks are ours. We put them first because they fit the brief, and we say so here so you can weigh them with that in mind. The other four are gifts you can make or find anywhere, and for some people they are the better choice.

09

A Handwritten Card

Best for: anyone, on its own or with any gift above

A card that names one specific thing a coworker did this year is the gift people keep after the cookies are gone. It costs a few dollars, takes ten minutes, and works where money gifts feel awkward, such as for a manager.

Its weakness is the same as its strength: it takes thought. A card that says only "Happy Holidays" is forgotten by January. The section on what to write below has three examples.

10

Homemade Baking

Best for: a small team you know well

Cookies or a loaf from your own kitchen cost little but your time and feel personal. They suit a team of four or five whose tastes and allergies you know.

For a bigger or newer team, homemade food carries a risk: nobody can check what is in it. If you bake, add a card listing every ingredient, and keep nuts out of it entirely. Our maple syrup uses post has ideas for baking with syrup.

11

A Mug They Will Use

Best for: a close work friend whose taste you know

A mug or a reusable travel cup is a classic coworker gift, and a good one gets used every day for years. It works best for someone you know well enough to pick the colour, size and style.

For anyone else, a mug is a gamble, because many office cupboards already hold spare mugs. Fill it with tea bags or a stroopwafel and it becomes a gift even if the mug stays in a drawer.

12

A Small Desk Plant

Best for: a coworker who sits by a window and is in most days

A small succulent or a pothos brightens a desk for years and costs about what a box of cookies does. Pick a plant that survives low light and missed waterings.

A plant is a poor fit for anyone who works from home most days or leaves for two weeks over the holidays, because it needs someone to look after it. In that case, food that keeps is the safer gift.

How to Choose a Gift for a Coworker

Start with the budget, then with how well you know the person. A gift exchange with a $10 limit points to one product from this list. A $25 limit allows a pair. A close work friend can get something personal, and a manager gets a gift from the group or a card.

What to leave off the list

Skip anything that guesses at a body or a habit: clothing sizes, scented lotions, diet or fitness products. Skip alcohol unless you know the person drinks, since some coworkers do not for health, faith or recovery. Skip gag gifts that could embarrass someone in front of the team.

When in doubt, give something that gets eaten or drunk within a month. It leaves nothing behind for the recipient to store, and it is hard to get wrong.

If you are the manager

A manager in Canada who gives the team gifts as the employer should know the Canada Revenue Agency's rule. Non-cash gifts and awards to an employee are not a taxable benefit up to a combined $500 a year, while cash and near-cash gifts, such as most gift cards, are taxable (Canada Revenue Agency, 2026). A box of food for each person stays well inside that line.

What to Write on the Card

Name one thing the person did this year and say what it meant for you. Two or three sentences is enough, and specific beats long. Three examples:

"Thank you for covering my shifts in August. I came back to a clean inbox and I have not forgotten it. Happy holidays."

"Our Monday meetings run on your coffee and your jokes. Here is something for the coffee. Merry Christmas."

"You taught me the new system when nobody else had time. I hope 2027 is a good one for you."
