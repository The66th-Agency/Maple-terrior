Gluten-Free Maple Cake Recipes Worth Baking

By Maple Terroir March 4, 2025 3 min read

Below are gluten-free maple cake recipes from other bakers, each linked to the baker's original recipe. Some of these cakes feed a crowd and one makes a single serving in a mug.

Every recipe on this list uses pure maple syrup. For the strongest maple flavor, use a Dark, Robust Taste maple syrup, such as our maple leaf bottle. If you bake for someone with celiac disease, check that every ingredient you buy, the oat flour included, is labeled gluten-free.

Maple Walnut Layer Cake

Dr. Rachel Pauls makes this low-FODMAP maple walnut layer cake with a gluten-free flour blend and finishes it with a maple buttercream. It is also dairy-free. Get the full recipe on Dr. Rachel Pauls' site.

Maple Almond Spice Cake

Lottie at Run On Veg bakes this spice cake with almond flour and oat flour and sweetens it with maple syrup. Get the full recipe on Run On Veg.

Vegan Oat Flour Cake

The Conscious Plant Kitchen makes this vegan cake with homemade oat flour and mashed banana, sweetened with maple syrup and coconut sugar. Get the full recipe on The Conscious Plant Kitchen.

Maple Apple Cake With No Refined Sugar

Sara Tso at Matchbox Kitchen makes this sliced apple cake with almond flour and gluten-free oat flour and sweetens it with maple syrup instead of refined sugar. It is also dairy-free. Get the full recipe on Matchbox Kitchen.

Maple Walnut Bundt Cake

Don Baiocchi, the Paleo Gluten-Free Guy, makes this bundt cake with almond flour and arrowroot starch and puts maple syrup in both the filling and the icing. It is also dairy-free. Get the full recipe on Paleo Gluten-Free Guy.

Maple Mug Cake

Alyssa Brantley at Everyday Maven makes this single-serving cake in a mug in the microwave, with coconut flour and pure maple syrup. It is also nut-free. Get the full recipe on Everyday Maven.

Maple Carrot Cake

From Scratch Fast makes this carrot cake with almond flour and maple syrup, spiced with cinnamon, cardamom, nutmeg and ginger, and tops it with a maple cream cheese frosting. Get the full recipe on From Scratch Fast.

Maple Cake With Cream Cheese Frosting

Mia Zarlengo at Gathered Nutrition bakes this square maple cake in an 8 by 8 inch pan with almond flour and oat flour, sweetens it with maple syrup and coconut sugar, and tops it with cream cheese frosting. Get the full recipe on Gathered Nutrition.

Pumpkin Cake With Maple Cream Cheese Frosting

Calla Ramont at Calla's Clean Eats makes her mom's pumpkin cake with a one-to-one gluten-free baking flour and tops it with a maple cream cheese frosting. Get the full recipe on Calla's Clean Eats.

To choose a syrup for any of these cakes, read our guide to the best maple syrup for baking. If you want to bake with maple sugar instead, our guide to maple sugar explains what it is and how to use it.

FAQs

Q1. What are some key ingredients for making gluten-free maple cakes? The bakers above use almond flour, oat flour, coconut flour, arrowroot starch or a gluten-free all-purpose flour blend, along with pure maple syrup. Some add applesauce or mashed banana for moisture.

Q2. How long do gluten-free maple cakes stay fresh? It depends on the recipe, so follow the storage notes in each baker's original recipe.

Q3. Can I make a vegan maple cake? Yes. The oat flour cake from The Conscious Plant Kitchen is vegan.

Q4. Is there a quick maple cake for when I am short on time? Yes. The maple mug cake from Everyday Maven makes one serving in the microwave.
