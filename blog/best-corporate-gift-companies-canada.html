Best Corporate Gift Companies in Canada for Client Gifts

Seven Canadian corporate gift companies compared on what a buyer has to know before ordering: the minimum order, the price per gift, how much of the gift is made in Canada, logo options, shipping to many addresses and lead time. Every fact comes from each company's own website, read on November 6, 2026, and every company is scored on the same seven criteria, weighted for a buyer of food and client gifts.

By Maple Terroir 13 min read
Published November 6, 2026 Last updated November 12, 2026

For food and client gifts, the best corporate gift company in Canada is Maple Terroir, with the top score on these criteria, 20 of 20. A complete gift starts at $5.99, a 50 mL maple leaf bottle of pure maple syrup in a case of 48, case prices from 12 to 48 are published online, and its organic maple syrups are certified by Ecocert, Canada Organic and USDA Organic.

Cheerfetti Gift Co. comes second at 14, with small gifts from Canadian and local makers, and Black Bow Gift Co. third at 12. If every gift has to carry your logo, Black Bow Gift Co. and Baskits publish logo pricing, which Maple Terroir does not; the "How to choose" section matches each company to the order it suits.

Prices are in Canadian dollars. Every price and fact comes from the company's own website on November 6, 2026, named under each breakdown, and may change after that date.

What Each Criterion Means

  1. Price per gift, counts double2 points if a complete gift costs under $15. 1 point for $15 to $50. 0 points if the lowest gift costs more than $50.
  2. Case prices published, counts double2 points for exact per-unit prices at set quantities. 1 point for a published bulk discount rate or bulk shipping rate. 0 points when every volume price is by quote.
  3. Made in Canada, counts double2 points if the company sells its own products, made or produced in Canada. 1 point if it curates other makers' products from Canada. 0 points if the website does not say.
  4. Certified organic2 points for products certified organic by a named certifier. 1 point for organic ingredients stated without a certifier. 0 points if not stated on the pages we read.
  5. Google rating2 points for 4.8 stars or more on the company's own Google business profile. 1 point for 4.5 to 4.7. 0 points below 4.5 or when no profile matched.
  6. Lead time2 points if a ready gift leaves within 3 business days. 1 point for within 10. 0 points for longer or not stated.
  7. Minimum order2 points if one gift can be ordered online. 1 point for a minimum of 12 or fewer. 0 points for a minimum of 25 or more.
  8. Listed, not scored: logo branding and many addressesEach breakdown says whether a company adds your logo and ships one order to many addresses. They are not scored because this list weighs what a food gift costs and contains; if either decides your order, the "How to choose" section names who offers it.

No gifts were ordered for this comparison, so the scores cover what each company publishes, and say nothing about the quality of the gifts themselves.

Maple Terroir is a maple company and a family business since 1978. Its maple syrup is pure, with no additives, and the shop also sells maple syrup stroopwafels, maple leaf shortbread, Maple Ceylon Tea, a maple roast coffee and boxed gift sets. Most products come in cases of 12, 24, 36 and 48 with the price per unit shown before you order, which is the clearest volume pricing of the seven companies here and the reason it scores highest. Two things it does not publish: logo branding, and one online order shipped to many addresses. A company sending a gift to each client's office can order to one place and send them on, or email the store first. For a larger order, email info@mapleterroir.com with the quantity, the date and the destination, and the reply includes pricing and lead times. Maple Terroir stroopwafels were supplied to the TED Conference in Vancouver in 2024, 2025 and 2026, as the TED post shows.

Delivery: each order is packed within 1 to 3 business days. Standard delivery then takes 3 to 9 business days for $19.99, Express takes 1 to 2 business days for $39.99, and orders of $99 or more ship free to the ten provinces.

Indigenous Box is an Indigenous-woman-owned company in Treaty 6 territory, near Edmonton, that brings gifts from Indigenous businesses and artists to more buyers. It says more than 1,200 organizations have ordered from it. Its ready-made boxes run from $32 to $199, they ship in 1 to 3 business days, and one online checkout can send boxes to many addresses.

How to Choose a Corporate Gift Company

Start with the one thing your order cannot do without. Each card names that need and the company on this list that answers it best, from its own published facts.

The lowest cost for 50 people or more

A case price you can see before ordering, and a complete gift under $10.

Maple Terroir: 48 maple leaf bottles at $5.99 each, $287.52 in all. See corporate gifts.

A logo on every gift

Branding that is priced on the website, not only on request.

Black Bow Gift Co., from $1.30 per gift, or Baskits, $5 per logo belly band.

A gift at each client's own address

One order that ships to many addresses, without a separate checkout for each.

Black Bow Gift Co. and Indigenous Box online, or Baskits by spreadsheet for more than 10 addresses.

The company's own products, made in Canada

A gift under the name of the company you buy from, made in Canada, instead of a box curated from other brands.

Maple Terroir for food, SOJA&CO. for candles from 25 units.

Gifts from Indigenous makers

A gift that supports Indigenous businesses and artists.

Indigenous Box, with boxes from $32 that ship in 1 to 3 business days.

A designed, premium box

Custom packaging, engraving or embroidery, and someone to handle the whole project.

Willows Box & Co., from 12 boxes with 4 to 6 weeks' notice.

Two questions settle most orders. First, who is the gift for? A gift for a client represents your company, so the presentation and a card with your name carry more weight. A gift for every employee is usually about the number of people and the price per person, and food that gets used up suits a team you know mostly from work, as the Christmas gifts for coworkers guide explains. Second, does every gift go to one office or to many homes? That decides whether the many-addresses criterion matters to you at all.

If you know the company but not yet the gift, a separate list of corporate gift ideas made in Canada sorts sixteen ideas by who they suit and what they cost. If you are a store buying cases to resell, the Maple Terroir maple syrup wholesale page covers case pricing and terms.

4 to 6 weeks ahead

Willows Box & Co. needs 4 to 6 weeks for a custom gift design, and Indigenous Box quotes 2 to 4 weeks for custom and corporate orders.

3 weeks ahead

Black Bow Gift Co. needs at least 3 weeks to put your brand on the products in a gift, and Baskits adds 1 to 3 weeks for a logo ribbon.

By December 10

Order from Maple Terroir by December 10 for Christmas delivery to any province. Cases of 12 to 48 are priced online, so there is no quote to wait for.

By December 11

Indigenous Box recommends ordering by December 11 for standard online orders, earlier for Atlantic Canada and rural, remote or northern addresses.
