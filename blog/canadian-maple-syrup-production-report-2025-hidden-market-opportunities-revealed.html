Canadian Maple Syrup Production in 2024

By Maple Terroir March 4, 2025 4 min read

Current State of Canadian Maple Syrup Production

Canadian maple syrup production set a record in 2024. Canadian producers harvested 19.9 million gallons of maple syrup, up 91.3 percent from 2023, according to Statistics Canada.

Production by Province in 2024

Quebec drove the increase. The province's producers collected 18 million gallons, up 8.7 million gallons (92.2 percent) from 2023. The bigger harvest helped refill the global strategic reserve, which had fallen to its lowest level in 16 years in 2023 (Statistics Canada).

That reserve was also the target of the 2011 to 2012 theft of C$18.7 million of syrup.

Ontario's maple producers harvested 607,000 gallons in 2024, up 20.9 percent from 2023. New Brunswick's production more than doubled (up 128.8 percent) to 1.2 million gallons, and Nova Scotia's grew by almost one third to 43,000 gallons (Statistics Canada).

Canadian producers also exported 8.4 million gallons of maple products in the first three quarters of 2024, up 8.8 percent from the same period in 2023 (Statistics Canada).

Weather and Sap Flow

Each season depends on the weather. Sap flows when nights freeze and days warm above 0°C, and the freeze and thaw cycle builds the pressure inside the tree that pushes sap out.

Quebec Maple Syrup Production Leadership

Quebec accounts for about 90 percent of Canadian production, and 55.6 million taps produce sap in Quebec sugar bushes today (Quebec Maple Syrup Producers).

Market Share and Production Statistics

Quebec's maple industry contributes $1.1 billion to Canada's gross domestic product, according to Quebec Maple Syrup Producers. Quebec's 2024 harvest reached a record 239 million pounds, an average of 4.47 pounds per tap.

According to Quebec Maple Syrup Producers, Quebec's maple sector:

  • Creates the equivalent of 12,600 full-time jobs

  • Generates $235 million in tax revenue for governments in Quebec and the rest of Canada

  • Exports 85 percent of its maple syrup to 69 countries

In 2025, 1,609 Quebec maple companies were certified organic, 20 percent of all operations (Quebec Maple Syrup Producers).

Strategic Reserve Management

Quebec Maple Syrup Producers (QMSP) manages the Global Strategic Maple Syrup Reserve, which it uses to steady supply and prices. The reserve has three warehouses (Quebec Maple Syrup Producers):

  1. The Laurierville Plant and Warehouse, used for storage and pasteurization:

    • Covers 268,000 square feet

    • Holds 55 million pounds, or 90,000 barrels

  2. The Plessisville Warehouse, used for storage only:

    • Covers 104,000 square feet

    • Holds 52 million pounds, or 95,000 barrels

  3. The Saint-Antoine-de-Tilly Warehouse, used for storage only:

    • Covers 75,000 square feet

    • Holds 26 million pounds, or 33,000 barrels

Together the three warehouses can store 133 million pounds of maple syrup, or 218,000 barrels. At full capacity, the reserve would be worth $400 million (Quebec Maple Syrup Producers).

When stocks decline, QMSP can issue additional quotas to producers, and when inventories grow too large, it can offer incentives to buyers (Quebec Maple Syrup Producers).

The record 2024 harvest helped refill the reserve after it fell to its lowest level in 16 years in 2023 (Statistics Canada).

QMSP says Quebec will need to add another 120 million taps by 2080 to meet demand, and the province has 178 million potential taps ready for production (Quebec Maple Syrup Producers).

FAQs

Q1. How much maple syrup does Canada produce annually? In 2024, Canadian maple syrup production reached a record 19.9 million gallons, with Quebec accounting for 18 million gallons of that total (Statistics Canada). Canada is the world's leading producer.

Q2. How does Canada manage its maple syrup supply? The Global Strategic Maple Syrup Reserve, managed by Quebec Maple Syrup Producers, helps stabilize the market. It can hold 133 million pounds across three warehouses, which lets producers regulate supply and steady prices.

Q3. Which provinces produce the most maple syrup after Quebec? In 2024, New Brunswick was Canada's second-largest producer, with 1.2 million gallons, and Ontario the third, with 607,000 gallons (Statistics Canada).
