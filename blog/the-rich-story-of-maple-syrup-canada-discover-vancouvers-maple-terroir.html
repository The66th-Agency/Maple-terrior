Maple Terroir: A Vancouver Family Selling Quebec Maple Since 1978

By Maple Terroir December 15, 2024 2 min read

Maple Terroir is a family business in Vancouver. Wayne and Kaori Lytton founded it in 1978, their son Shawn runs it today as CEO, and our maple syrup comes from one family farm in Quebec’s Appalachian Mountains.

The Family Behind Maple Terroir

We buy all of our maple syrup from that one farm, so every bottle we sell is single-origin.

Our story page tells how three generations of our family have run the company since 1978.

Besides maple syrup, we sell:

  • Cookies: maple sugar cookies, maple leaf shortbread cookies
  • Tea and coffee: maple tea, maple coffee
  • Stroopwafels: maple stroopwafels

Our maple syrup is pure maple syrup with nothing added. How much sap it takes to make a gallon depends on how sweet the sap is, and our guide to the Rule of 86 explains how to work that out.

The trees are in Quebec. The boxes go out from Vancouver.

We work with one family farm in the Appalachian Mountains and pack every order here in Vancouver. Local pickup is free at checkout.

FAQs

Q1. What is Canadian maple syrup? Canadian maple syrup is maple sap boiled down, with nothing added. Its flavor depends on the grade, which reflects when in the season the sap ran.

Q2. Is Maple Terroir maple syrup organic? Our organic 250 mL bottle and First Tap Nouveau are certified by Ecocert, Canada Organic and USDA Organic. Our maple leaf bottle is not organic.

Q3. Is Vancouver a maple syrup producer? No. Vancouver is not a maple syrup production region, but Maple Terroir is based there. We buy our syrup from one family farm in Quebec’s Appalachian Mountains, and we have sold maple products in Canada since 1978, and in markets such as Japan and South Korea.
