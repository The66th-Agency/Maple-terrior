Maple Sugar: What It Is and How to Use It

By Maple Terroir July 20, 2025 2 min read

What Exactly is Maple Sugar?

Maple sugar is a natural sweetener made by boiling pure maple syrup until it crystallizes into golden granules. It tastes of caramel with a hint of vanilla. You can find ours in the shop as Organic Pure Maple Sugar.

For baking ideas, our roundup of gluten-free maple cake recipes links to cakes from other bakers.

Key Features of Maple Sugar

  • 100% pure and single-ingredient
  • Use in place of white or brown sugar
  • Made from organic pure maple syrup
  • Granulated form for easy storage and use

How to Use Maple Sugar

Versatility in the Kitchen

You can use maple sugar in both sweet and savory recipes.

Home cooks ready to try it can pick up our Organic Pure Maple Sugar.

  • Add depth to cookies, cakes, and pastries with a natural caramel sweetness
  • Use as a rub for meats or vegetables to achieve a delicate glaze on the grill
  • Sweeten coffee, tea and cocktails

Culinary Inspirations with Maple Sugar

Baked Goods & Desserts

Maple sugar brings a caramel sweetness to cookies, cakes, frostings and fillings. Use it in place of white or brown sugar in your favorite recipes, and adjust to taste.

Grilling and BBQ

Combine it with smoked paprika and chili powder for a sweet and savory rub on ribs, steaks or roasted vegetables.

Beverages and Cocktails

To sweeten cocktails, dissolve maple sugar in water and add cinnamon, ginger or citrus zest.

Sprinkle it, bake with it, stir it into coffee.

Practical Storage and Usage Tips

Proper storage keeps maple sugar loose and full of flavor:

  • Keep in airtight containers to avoid clumping from moisture.
  • If clumping occurs, gently break apart using a fork or pulse in a food processor.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight for optimal freshness.

Comparing Maple Sugar to Other Sweeteners

Sweetener Flavor Profile Cooking Uses
Maple Sugar Rich caramel, subtle vanilla Baking, grilling, drinks
White Sugar Neutral sweetness General cooking, baking
Honey Floral sweetness Baking, beverages
