How We Scored Them

We picked companies that ship Christmas gift baskets to buyers anywhere in the provinces and appear on the first page of Google Canada for "christmas gift baskets canada", "best gift baskets canada" or "gift basket companies canada" on November 6, 2026. A local shop that delivers only in one city is not on this list; our city pages, such as gift baskets in Toronto and gift baskets in Vancouver, cover local delivery.

For each company we read its Christmas collection, one basket priced near $75 to $100, its shipping and corporate pages, and its Google listing. We did not order from the other companies, so nothing here grades taste or packing; the scores grade what each company publishes, which is what a buyer can check before paying.

Starting price

2 points if the cheapest Christmas gift starts at $60 or less, 1 point from $61 to $80, 0 above $80.

What is inside

2 points if the product page lists every item with its size, 1 if it names the items without sizes, 0 if it only describes them.

Canadian contents

2 points if the company says its baskets hold Canadian-made or Canadian-sourced goods and the basket we read has no imported finished product, 1 if the claim covers some items or one collection, 0 if it is not stated.

Christmas delivery

1 point for delivery to all ten provinces and three territories, plus 1 for a published 2026 Christmas order-by date.

Corporate orders

2 points for published volume pricing or logo branding, 1 for bulk orders handled by email or a form, 0 if not offered.

Google rating

2 points for 4.8 stars or more, 1 for 4.4 to 4.7, 0 below 4.4, read from each company's Google listing.

How to Choose a Christmas Gift Basket Company

Start with the person and the address, then the budget. The table above shows the scores; this is the short version by situation.

Someone who loves Canadian food

Choose a box where every item is Canadian. Ours are all maple, from a $75 Maple Treat Home Set to the $240 Mega Combo; Saul Good fills its boxes from small Canadian makers.

A client list or a whole team

Look for one invoice, many addresses and branding: My Baskets (from 10 baskets), Saul Good (branding from 25), Baskits (a logo ribbon from 25). For ten or more of our sets, email info@mapleterroir.com and we reply with pricing.

A gift that has to arrive tomorrow

Pick a company with same-day or overnight delivery where the person lives: Hazelton's in the Greater Toronto Area and the Lower Mainland, The Gourmet Gifts overnight, The Sweet Basket in Montreal.

Someone in Yukon, the Northwest Territories or Nunavut

Order early from a company that ships there: Hazelton's, Baskits, My Baskets (non-perishable only, by November 30), The Gourmet Gifts, Saul Good or Peter and Paul's. We do not ship to the territories.

A wine or spirits basket

Alcohol follows provincial rules: Saul Good ships wine only to British Columbia and Ontario, My Baskets needs an adult to sign at a home address, and Hazelton's includes alcohol only where it is allowed.

A smaller budget

Hazelton's starts at $29.99 and The Gourmet Gifts at $49.99. For one small Canadian gift per person instead of a basket, see our Christmas gifts for coworkers from $7.99.

Basket or box?

A wicker basket looks good on a doorstep in the same city. Across the country, a box protects the contents better: Saul Good and our own sets ship only in boxes, and Peter and Paul's says a gift sent outside the Greater Toronto Area may arrive laid flat. If the gift travels far, a box is the safer choice.

Is a Canadian-made basket worth it?

For a gift that says Canada, yes, and the label tells you whether you are getting one. Of the eight companies, only Saul Good and we describe our gifts as Canadian as a rule; the others mix Canadian and imported goods or do not say.
