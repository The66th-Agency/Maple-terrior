Three certifications for our organic syrup.

Ecocert, Canada Organic and USDA Organic certify our organic maple syrups: the Organic Pure Maple Syrup 250ml and the Organic First Tap Nouveau. Our maple leaf bottles and 100 mL jug are not organic.

Ecocert

International Organic Certification

Ecocert is an organic certification body founded in France in 1991. Ecocert certifies our organic maple syrup, including the First Tap Nouveau. Learn more about our organic maple syrup.

Canada Organic

Canadian Organic Standards

The Canada Organic logo is regulated by the Canadian Food Inspection Agency (CFIA) under the Canadian Organic Standards. Our organic maple syrup is certified to this standard.

USDA Organic

National Organic Program

Earning the USDA Organic seal requires meeting the National Organic Program (NOP) standards, which regulate everything from soil quality to pest management.

What our certifications mean for you.

For each certification, an independent body inspected how our organic syrup is produced and confirmed that it meets that organic standard. We hold the three certifications for our organic syrups only.
